What the labels mean

Everything below is a suggestion from your assistant until a person confirms it.

What has to change at this step

New step A step that doesn't exist today has to be added here. Product and engineering work.
Changed The step stays, but what happens inside it changes — the data, the timing, or who does it.
Wording The process is identical. Only what we say to the customer changes — copy, emails, help pages.
No change This step is unaffected.
Nothing selected means the assistant couldn't tell, and nobody has decided yet.

How sure the assistant is

Stated The change model says this explicitly. The quote underneath points at the wording.
Implied The assistant reasoned this out. Worth checking the reasoning holds.
Check The assistant doesn't know. Someone who knows the change has to decide.
A high number of Implied usually means the change model is written too abstractly, not that the assistant is guessing badly.

Who has to do something

Customer-facing What the customer sees, does or receives at this step changes.
Advisors Support advisors need new knowledge, scripts or training.
Brokers / TPIs Third-party intermediaries have to do something differently.
Billing ops The billing team's process or checks change.
Systems A system change is needed — data, configuration or code.
These are about who must act, not who is present. Click any tag in the table to filter by it.

Review state

Awaiting review Nobody has looked at this yet.
AI suggested The assistant has proposed an answer. Confirm it or pick a different one.
Reviewed A person decided. If they overruled the assistant, the original suggestion is kept on record.
